Writing Functions

Functions and program structure.

WEEK 03

Copying Data

WEEK 04

Integer Arithmetic

Arithmetic operations and numerical computation.

WEEK 05

Decisions & Loops

Conditional execution, branching, and loops.

WEEK 06

Manipulating Bits

Develop the foundation needed to understand how software directly interacts with computer hardware.
