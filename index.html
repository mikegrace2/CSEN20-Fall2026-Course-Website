Course Roadmap

A ten-week journey through the foundations of embedded systems and ARM assembly programming.

Week 01

Introduction & Binary

Computer basics, number systems, and binary representation.

Week 02

Writing Functions

Functions, program structure, and low-level execution.

Week 03

Copying Data

Registers, memory, loading, storing, and moving data.

Week 04

Integer Arithmetic

Arithmetic operations and numerical computation.

Week 05

Decisions & Loops

Conditional execution, branching, and repetition.

Week 06

Manipulating Bits

Bitwise operations, masks, shifts, and binary logic.

Week 07

Multiply & Divide

Advanced multiplication and division techniques.

Week 08

Floating Point

IEEE floating point, FPU concepts, and emulation.

Week 09

Fixed Point & ARM64

Fixed-point real numbers and modern ARM architecture.

Week 10

64-Bit ARM

64-bit ARM programming and final course preparation.

Learning Outcomes

Build a Strong Low-Level Foundation

🖥️ Computer Architecture

Understand how processors, memory, registers, instructions, and data work together inside a computer system.

⚡ ARM Assembly Language

Write and understand low-level ARM programs using instructions, functions, arithmetic, branches, and loops.

🔧 Embedded Systems

Develop the foundation needed to understand how software directly interacts with hardware and embedded devices.
